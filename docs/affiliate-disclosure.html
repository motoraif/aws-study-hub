🤝 Affiliate Disclosure

How we keep AWS Study Hub free, and our promise to recommend honestly.

AWS Study Hub is free and open source. To help cover hosting and development, some outbound links to third-party courses and tools are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Our commitments

  • Official and free first. We always point you to official AWS resources (Skill Builder, exam guides, whitepapers) and free options before any paid one.
  • Honest recommendations. We only link to courses and tools we believe are genuinely useful. A commission never changes what we recommend or how we rank it.
  • No extra cost to you. Affiliate links never increase the price you pay.
  • You are never required to use them. You can search for any resource directly instead of using our links.

Which links are affiliate links?

Primarily some links to paid third-party courses (for example, Udemy instructors such as Stephane Maarek) and similar learning tools. Links to official AWS pages and free resources are not affiliate links.

Programs we participate in

We participate in affiliate programs via partners such as Impact.com (which powers the Udemy affiliate program). Participation may change over time.
